I architect high-performance digital products that operate at the intersection of engineering precision and design excellence. With deep expertise in modern React ecosystems and AI-augmented workflows, I build systems designed to scale from day one — not retrofitted under pressure six months after launch.

My first training wasn't in software — it was in Ayurvedic medicine, a discipline built entirely on systematic diagnostics. Before any treatment, you take a complete intake: history, symptoms, constitution, context. Only then do you prescribe. I approach every codebase the same way — I don't open a pull request before I understand the full system, the data flow, and the edge cases a less rigorous process would miss.

That habit of methodical intake is, I think, the real difference between code that survives a demo and code that survives production. It's slower at the start and faster everywhere after.
